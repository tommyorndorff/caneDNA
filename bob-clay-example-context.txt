This is the example taper from the Bob Clay spey rod building class on Anchored Outdoors.

So in point form
-This  is 1/2 of the dimension of the finished rod or you could say strip dimension
-Universal taper for a 12 foot rod.
-Taper starts at tip and is for every 5 inches of length
-Taper stops at top of grip say around 22 inches level after that.
-This is for a 6 sided rod at 60 degrees. If using the Morgan handmill which is at 61 1/2 degrees deduct 2.3 percent to end up with the proper  dimension
-This rod would be a 7/8 weight,for a line size up add 3 percent .For a line size down  deduct 3 percent from the figures.

In the first row, 144 is the number of inches from zero.
